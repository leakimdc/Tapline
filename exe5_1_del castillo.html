The Quiet Garden

The morning sun climbs slow and gold,
Across the fields both new and old,
The dew upon the grass does gleam,
Like scattered bits of silver dream.

A butterfly with wings of lace,
Drifts softly to a resting place,
While petals bloom in reds and blues,
And leaves are wet with morning dews.

The garden hums a gentle tune,
Beneath the watch of fading moon,
And I, content, sit still and see,
The quiet world that welcomes me.
