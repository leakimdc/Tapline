The History of Mandaue City College

The first Mandaue City College was established by Mandaue City's 10th Sangguniang Panlungsod's Ordinance No. 10-2005-324A on Sept. 27, 2005, later revised on Oct. 7, 2010 under Ord. No. 12-2010-568. Said ordinance was sponsored by SP Members Carlo Fortuna, and Jonas Cortes as co-sponsor. This was later revised on Oct. 7, 2010 by Ord. No. 12-2010-568, otherwise known as "The Mandaue City College Charter" when the latter became mayor in 2007 until 2024.

Founded by Mayor Teddy Ouano from the ground up, the city college started its 4-yr. course in Academic Year 2006-2007 and first Commencement Exercises, 1st Batch 2009-2010 on March 20, 2010. Shown on the first graduation day were the Faculty and Staff with guest speaker, Sen. Edgardo Angara (middle in white barong tagalog), Chairman, Senate Committee on Education, Republic of the Philippines. Front row, L-R: Dr. Edgar Agustilo, Dean, Teacher Education, 2006-2010 & the Faculty: Dr. Darwin Goron, Dr. Erickson Avendaño, Dr. Mila Cañete, Dr. Dan Salud, Dr. Editha Flores (now Dean, 2024) Mandaue City College - School of Teacher Education, Dr. Doreen Espina, VP-Academics, Dr. Pia Cortes, Guidance Services.

Only the legal Mandaue City College is supported by the OUANOs as the incoming mayor Jonkie Ouano vows to continue the projects of his predecessor, like the city college, and the city hospital, to name a few.
