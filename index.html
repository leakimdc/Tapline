History

Where Tapline came from.

In an emergency, every second counts, yet people often have to remember the right number for the place they are in and type it in by hand. Countries use different numbers, and some have separate ones for police, fire and ambulance.

Tapline started from a simple idea: set up the call once, then reach help with a single tap. The user picks a country, adds a personal contact for someone close, and saves the setup so nothing has to be adjusted again.
